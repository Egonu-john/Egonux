# KAMPALA PILOT ONBOARDING STANDARD

**Scope**: 50 Selected Kampala Cohort Participants  
**Framework**: ANOSA Sovereign Launch Standard (Phase 5)  
**Legal Standard**: Ugandan Data Protection and Privacy Act (2019)  
**Approving Authority**: Founder & CEO (John William Egonu)  

---

## 👥 1. Participant Journey Architecture

```
[ Invitation Code ] ──► [ Explicit Consent ] ──► [ Auth & Account Setup ]
                                                            │
[ Pilot Dashboard ] ◄── [ Non-Custodial Profile ] ◄──────────┘
```

### Step-by-Step Onboarding Flow:
1. **Controlled Invitation**: Issued via unique single-use token tied to participant contact record.
2. **Data Privacy Consent**: Explicit consent prompt detailing data collection boundaries under the Ugandan Data Protection and Privacy Act (2019).
3. **Account Provisioning**: Zero-trust authentication registration with multi-factor option.
4. **Profile & Sandbox Wallet**: Auto-provisioning of non-custodial EGONUX wallet address and sandbox ledger profile.
5. **Support Channel Access**: Direct access to Founder & CEO support protocol and fast-track feedback mechanism.

---

## 🔐 2. Privacy & Compliance Policies (Uganda Baseline)
- **Data Retention**: Pilot data encrypted at rest and in transit. Retained strictly during pilot duration.
- **Data Subject Rights**: Participants retain right to access, rectify, or request erasure of profile data.
- **Financial Sandbox Notice**: Real-money movement disabled. All ledger transactions utilize simulated/sandbox UGX assets.

---

## 🎯 3. Pilot Onboarding Acceptance Criteria
- 100% of participants must complete explicit consent before accessing features.
- Zero unauthorized account creation attempts permitted.
- Average onboarding completion time under 3 minutes.
